Definition of "Domestic/Overseas" in Journal History:

“境内”旅程指起点、终点、途经点全部位于中国内地或香港西九龙火车站的交通方式。除此之外为“境外”旅程。

"Domestic" journeys are modes of transport whose start, end, and via points are all located inside Mainland China or Hong Kong West Kowloon Railway Station. Journeys that are not "Domestic" are "Overseas".
